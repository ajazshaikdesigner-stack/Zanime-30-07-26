# ZANIME User Guide

Welcome to ZANIME, your AI-powered animation companion.

## First Steps
When you launch ZANIME, you will be greeted by the **Welcome Screen**. Here you can:
1. **Create a New Project**: Start a fresh canvas.
2. **Open Existing Project**: Load a `.zanime` file.
3. **Open Demo Project**: Launch the pre-bundled tutorial project to learn the ropes.

## Workspaces
ZANIME uses a flexible workspace layout. The left sidebar contains options to switch between modes:
- **Home**: Return to your welcome screen or manage projects.
- **Animation**: The primary workspace. Opens the Properties, Timeline, Project Explorer, and Console docks.

## Docks and Panels
You can detach and move docks anywhere on your screen:
- **Properties**: Context-sensitive properties of your current selection.
- **Timeline**: Manage layers and keyframes.
- **Project Explorer**: Browse your project assets.
- **Console**: View application logs.

## Saving
Hit `Ctrl+S` or `File > Save` to save your work. ZANIME saves files as compressed `.zanime` archives containing your metadata and assets.
